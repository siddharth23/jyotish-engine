# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

This project welcomes contributors regardless of background, nationality, belief, or level of
experience. Astrology attracts strong opinions in both directions; discussion of the subject
matter should stay technical and respectful.

Expected:

- Assume good faith.
- Critique code and calculations, not people.
- Accept that contributors hold varied views on astrology itself. Debating its validity is
  out of scope for this repository's issue tracker.

Not acceptable:

- Personal attacks, harassment, or discriminatory language.
- Dismissing contributors on the basis of their beliefs about astrology, in either direction.
- Publishing others' private information.

## Scope

Applies to all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions — and to public spaces
when representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report concerns by opening a confidential issue or contacting the maintainer directly.
Reports are handled discreetly. Maintainers may remove comments, close pull requests, or ban
contributors for behaviour they judge inappropriate.
